Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. መዝሙረ ዳዊት is designed as a fully local application for reading and listening to the Psalms of David in Amharic, Ge'ez, and English. The App does not require an account, and your personal information is not collected by us.
❖
§ 1

Information We Collect

መዝሙረ ዳዊት does not collect personal information. We do not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, password, or other personal details.

The App does not use analytics, advertising services, tracking technologies, or a remote user database.

§ 2

Local-Only Operation

The App is designed to work locally on your device. Its reading content, preferences, and supported audio functionality operate without requiring you to create an account or send personal information to us.

Nothing is sent to our servers

We do not operate a server that receives or stores your reading activity, selected language, prayer-day preferences, or other personal usage information.

§ 3

Audio & Offline Content

መዝሙረ ዳዊት provides Psalm content in Amharic, Ge'ez, and English, including audio for supported Ge'ez content.

  • Audio and reading content may be stored locally on your device for offline use.
  • Listening to audio does not require us to receive information about what you are listening to.
  • Your reading and listening activity is not sent to the developer.
§ 4

App Preferences

The App may store certain preferences locally on your device, such as language, appearance, or reading settings.

These preferences remain on your device and are not transmitted to us.

§ 5

Third-Party Services

The App does not intentionally integrate third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, authentication, or cloud-storage services that collect personal information from users.

❖

Private by Design

No account. No tracking. No advertising. No personal-data collection by the App.

§ 6

Data Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal user database maintained by us.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your own Android device and any data stored locally on it.

§ 7

Children's Privacy

መዝሙረ ዳዊት does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Since the App does not require an account or collect personal information, no personal profile is created for users.

§ 8

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality changes or if additional services are introduced.

Any changes will be published on this page together with a revised effective date.
